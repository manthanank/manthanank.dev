## Overview

Part of **TheLearningHubOrg** open-source initiative, **Learn System Design** is a comprehensive architectural playbook breaking down the design of large-scale distributed systems, high-throughput microservices, and fault-tolerant infrastructure.

## Key Curriculum Modules

- **System Design Fundamentals**: Latency vs throughput, SLA/SLO definition, back-of-the-envelope capacity estimations, and network protocols (HTTP/2, HTTP/3, gRPC, WebSockets).
- **Scalability & Partitioning**: Horizontal vs vertical scaling, consistent hashing, database sharding strategies, and multi-region read replicas.
- **Data Stores & Caching**: Cache invalidation patterns (Write-through, Write-back, Cache-aside), Redis clusters, ClickHouse columnar storage, and transactional consensus.
- **Reliability & Resilience**: Circuit breakers, exponential backoff with jitter, rate limiting algorithms (Token Bucket, Leaky Bucket), and zero-downtime deployment pipelines.
- **Real-World Architectural Blueprints**: Step-by-step system designs for URL shorteners, distributed telemetry tracing engines, ride-sharing geohash dispatchers, and global notification brokers.
